**How does the keyboard shortcut actually work?**

It’s dead simple. Here’s the step by step:

1. Highlight the sentence or phrase you want to rephrase in the WordPress editor. 2. Press the keyboard shortcut. The default is usually Ctrl+Shift+R on Windows or Cmd+Shift+R on Mac. But you can customize it in the extension settings. 3. A small popup appears with a rewritten version of your text. You can accept it, reject it, or ask for another option. 4. If you like what you see, click to replace the original. Done.

That’s it. No extra windows, no copy pasting, no logins. It’s like having a rephrase button built right into your brain.
